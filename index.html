The 1099 Radar

Free. A Google Sheets + Apps Script tool from Build It With AI, Episode 25. Paste your year's payment ledger and vendor list; it tells you which vendors need a 1099-NEC, which rent needs a 1099-MISC, which W-9s you're missing and what that exposure costs, which payments are the card processor's job (Form 1099-K) rather than yours, what it refused to read and why — and it re-adds the whole ledger to the cent before it will vouch for any of it.

  2. Extensions → Apps Script → paste radar.gs from the bundle → save → reload the sheet.
  3. Menu 1099 Radar → Build the report. Try the sample CSVs first.

The three prompts, exactly as sent

The bundle also ships both wrong builds — labeled NOT THE TOOL — plus the independent Python checker and the SPEC, so you can re-run the whole verification yourself.

Prompt 1 — the honest ask (produces the wreck) 
In this Google Sheet I have a "Payments" tab (Date, Vendor, Description, Category, Method, Amount) and a "Vendors" tab (Vendor, Entity type, W-9 on file, Email). Write an Apps Script that reads both and tells me which vendors need a 1099 for 2026, with each vendor's total.
Prompt 2 — the 2026 rules (still misses three) 
Rebuild it with the actual 2026 rules from the IRS Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC (the 12/2026 revision): the reporting threshold is $2,000 now, not $600. Payments made by credit card or through third-party settlement networks are reported by the processor on Form 1099-K, not by me — exclude them. Payments to corporations are exempt. Rent is not nonemployee compensation: it goes on a 1099-MISC, box 1. Count only payments dated in the tax year, and catch exact duplicate rows pasted twice.
Prompt 3 — the exceptions and the trust rules (the tool) 
Assume the ledger is dirty and every rule has an exception, and write the final version:
1. Legal services are NEVER exempt — the corporation exemption does not apply to payments for legal services.
2. Zelle is a bank transfer, not a third-party settlement network. Those payments are still mine to report. PayPal/Venmo/Square/card payments stay excluded.
3. A service vendor's "Parts & materials" lines count toward the 1099-NEC total.
4. Refuse any row you cannot read — bad date, bad amount, a vendor not in the Vendors tab, a negative amount — and print the row number and the reason.
5. Park exact duplicates (same vendor, amount, method, category within a settable window) and count them once, visibly.
6. Hold payments dated outside the tax year in their own bucket.
7. List every vendor that needs a form but has no W-9 on file, note that backup withholding (24%) applies until the W-9 lands, and price the exposure from the IRS penalty table for returns due in 2027 ($340 per form after August 1 — charged once for the IRS copy and again for the payee copy).
8. Re-add every parseable dollar into the buckets and print DO NOT TRUST THIS REPORT if the sum misses by a cent or more than 5% of rows were refused.
All money in integer cents. Settings (threshold, taxYear, penaltyTier, dupDays) read from a Settings tab. No network calls of any kind.
